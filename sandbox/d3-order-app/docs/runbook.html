1. What it does

A customer places an order made of sale lines. Each line validates the customer, product, available stock and the customer's credit limit, takes an update lock on the product to decrement stock, prices the line, and accumulates the order subtotal, 15% VAT and total. Goods can be received, orders settled and cancelled, and goods returned as credit notes. Every stock movement is journalled so stock and balances can be reconciled.

Business rules. VAT fixed at 15%; prices come from the product record; stock can never go negative; a sale that would exceed the credit limit is refused before any write; a paid order cannot be cancelled (credit it instead); cancelling an order whose product was removed reports OK.PARTIAL instead of silently skipping the stock restore.

Dictionary items are built by D3CODE.DICTGEN; AQL reserved words are avoided (the total field is AMOUNT, not TOTAL). Indexes: D3CODE.PRODUCTS A1 (description) and D3CODE.ORDERS A2 (customer).

Operational cautions. Recreating a file (DELETE-FILE + CREATE-FILE) drops its dictionary and indexes — run D3CODE.DICTGEN and recreate the indexes afterwards. Sales, receipts, credits and settlements change stock and balances permanently: point tests at the D3CODE.* namespace only. D3 has no TCL CALL; run a catalogued main program instead of calling a subroutine from the command line.
